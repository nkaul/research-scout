Evidence boundaries

  • Everything shown is saved evidence from this repository. The page calls no model, index or API.
  • "Responses" are retrieved records, not LLM answers. Similarity scores are not probabilities, and synthetic relevance labels are not expert judgments.
  • Only measured pools appear: test, validation and index documents. Unmeasured settings stay locked.
  • Macro recall averages coverage per question; micro recall is overall useful-option coverage; precision is the useful share of the returned list; MRR is the reciprocal rank of the first relevant result.
  • mxbai was selected at 512 on validation and differs in capacity, loss and batch size. OpenAI rows are unchanged full-text references.
  • HNSW neighbor overlap is agreement with exact search, not domain recall. Exact timing includes the full sort; HNSW timing covers native lookup only.
