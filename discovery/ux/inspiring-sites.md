# Inspiring Sites

**Updated October 29, 2026 · 30 new light-interface references · 15 from each of two UX research agents**

## Current direction

The user likes the light, slick feel of [starter.cal.com](https://starter.cal.com/), but wants a more complete workspace: **a modern left sidebar, richer settings, useful filters and view controls, and room to inspect content**. The research is now framework-agnostic. React/Next.js eligibility is no longer the filter for this list; Research Scout's implementation stack is unchanged.

The links below lead to specific **light application screens**, rather than a product's potentially dark marketing homepage. Every counted reference has a publisher-provided light UI image actually inspected by its assigned agent. Exact screenshot dates, native/desktop scope, mixed chrome and partial views are qualified in the tables and detailed reports. These are design judgments, not a usability ranking or a selected product design.

## What this suggests for Research Scout

- **Light shell:** near-white reading surfaces, a slightly tinted sidebar, clear selected states and one controlled accent. Compare small colored object/provider marks with simple monochrome icons.
- **A useful left bar:** separate core destinations from pinned/recent research and larger collections. Keep labels readable and make additional navigation discoverable without filling the first screen with every feature.
- **Richer controls close to their object:** put result filters, sorting and display choices above results; keep source inspection and human notes beside the selected source; group settings by application/workspace, provider connection and current run.
- **Depth with restraint:** learn from settings pages with many options and clear sections. Additional controls should correspond to real capabilities; these references do not add builders, collaboration, administration suites or unrelated dashboards to Increment 1.

## Evidence, access and qualification

The two agents inspected **30 distinct light UI references**, 15 each, from official product/help/changelog images or animation frames, and read the surrounding controls documentation. Root independently inspected Attio and both Dify views, and checked examples that needed exclusion. A current page can contain an older screenshot; those dates are retained. Native references such as Craft are labeled. Directus has a narrow dark rail; Sanity has a decorative dark exterior; some WorkOS images include modal dimming. They are useful qualified comparisons, not all-white-shell recommendations. Lower-fit control references remain below the strongest visual matches.

No authenticated app walkthrough, responsive/accessibility test or account creation was performed. Images were viewed as published references, not copied into the app. Dark examples from Dovetail, Anytype, Superlist and Asana, and insufficient full-workspace evidence from several admin candidates, were screened out of the counted sets. This does not claim those products lack light modes; it means the inspected assets were unsuitable for this pass.

The user's positive Cal.com starter reference is retained as the visual baseline. Linear's [documented light-theme preference](https://linear.app/docs/account-preferences) remains relevant to its workspace structure, but its dark marketing/UI pictures are not the primary viewing links here.
