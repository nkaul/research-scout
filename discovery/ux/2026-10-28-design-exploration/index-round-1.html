Three proposals for 2027
A more distinctive research workspace

Three ways to
follow the evidence.

More personality. Clearer hierarchy. A workspace that makes finding, reading and keeping useful sources feel considered.

These are standalone HTML design studies with fictional demo records and simulated interactions. All three retain one selected discovery provider and human review. They propose a 2027 direction using practices available today; they are not future rankings or production releases.

01
Expressive · confident · welcoming

Cobalt Studio

Material-inspired tonal surfaces, a bold blue question canvas and purposeful rounded forms. A familiar workspace with a more distinctive identity.
