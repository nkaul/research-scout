# Research Scout × HabitQuest hybrid

November 9, 2026 · standalone prototype · fictional data

This follow-up starts with Research Scout's selected Dub-inspired organization: white navigation, a compact question composer, restrained type, a source index beside a persistent reader, and dark primary actions. It adapts the tested workbench preview's local interactions without connecting to any application service.

HabitQuest contributes a small set of accents: lavender selection and reader artwork, deep violet focus/links, a softly rounded composer, gold shortlist indicators and a single colorful book illustration in the reader. The main canvas and reading copy stay neutral. There are no game, character, family or reward features.

The practical controls remain source selection, notes, metadata, a three-source shortlist, filtering, density preferences and a clearly illustrative arrival replay. Local controls do not implement new provider or model behavior. Nothing in this design changes Research Scout's implementation or stack.

The full HabitQuest previews remain available beside this hybrid for comparison. Artwork attribution remains in `assets/LICENSE` and `assets/provenance.json`. The hybrid uses existing local font fallbacks and adds no dependencies or remote asset requests.
