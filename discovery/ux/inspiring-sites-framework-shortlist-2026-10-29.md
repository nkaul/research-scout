# Inspiring Sites

**Research Scout UX research · checked October 29, 2026**

A curated set of React/Next.js references for a more considered Research Scout UI. These are design recommendations, not an objective ranking or a new component-library decision. Prefer studying actual product screens and public demos over copying a marketing hero.

## Start with these three

1. **[Linear](https://linear.app/)** — the strongest reference here for a coherent, information-dense working environment. Open its [2026 interface-refresh study](https://linear.app/now/behind-the-latest-design-refresh) alongside the product site: look at the alignment of headers, restrained navigation, source-like rows and predictable actions.
2. **[Resend](https://resend.com/)** — study its visual restraint, sharp type hierarchy and carefully grouped product controls. The [light-theme design study](https://resend.com/blog/introducing-light-mode) is a useful **January 2024 historical example** of adapting a strong brand to a readable work surface.
3. **[Dub Links](https://dub.co/links)** — inspect the public link-builder and management imagery for clear forms, compact metadata, preview placement and action hierarchy. [Dub Analytics](https://dub.co/analytics) adds examples of progressive disclosure; borrow the organization, not unrelated charts or marketing metrics.

## How to use this list

- **Composition first:** compare Linear's working hierarchy, Dub's editing/preview relationship and Liveblocks' reading-and-annotation arrangement. Choose the composition before picking a palette.
- **Identity second:** compare Resend's restrained product presentation with Raycast's stronger visual personality. Keep clear text, usable source rows and human review prominent in either direction.
- **Review real states:** inspect selected/unselected sources, pending operations, missing metadata, personal notes and narrow layouts. A polished homepage does not establish that those states work well.

Framework evidence is first-party documentation/source or a directly observed response from the site's own server; technology-detector websites were not used as proof. Public pages and publisher-provided UI references were reviewed without signing in, creating accounts or making purchases. Browser surfaces were unavailable for a live interaction review, so this list does **not** claim full app walkthroughs, accessibility audits or measured usability. No product screens/assets were copied into the application, and none of the existing prototypes or production files was changed.
